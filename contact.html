Frequently Asked Questions

What are your business hours?

+

Our main offices are open Monday through Friday, 9AM-6PM in their respective time zones. We also offer virtual consultations outside of business hours by appointment.

How quickly will I hear back?

+

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please call us directly at 1-800-VITAHEALTH or use our live chat feature.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

+

Absolutely! We offer virtual consultations via video call, phone, or email for your convenience. You can book a virtual appointment during our contact process.

What if I need immediate assistance?

+

For immediate assistance, please call 1-800-VITAHEALTH or use our live chat feature during business hours. Members have 24/7 priority support access.

Can I cancel or modify my subscription?

+

Yes! You can modify or cancel your subscription anytime. Simply log into your account or contact our support team and we'll process your request immediately.

Is there a free consultation available?

+

Yes! We offer a complimentary 30-minute consultation with one of our coaches. This is a great opportunity to discuss your goals and see how we can help you.
