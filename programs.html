Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch programs after starting?

+

Absolutely! We understand that your needs may change. You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch programs at any time. Our team will help ensure a smooth transition.

Do you offer refunds?

+

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all programs. If you're not satisfied within the first month, we'll provide a full refund, no questions asked.

What if I have dietary restrictions or medical conditions?

+

Our programs can be completely customized for dietary restrictions and medical conditions. During your onboarding, we'll gather detailed health information to tailor your plan.

How do I get started?

+

Simply choose a program and click "Start Your Journey" or "Learn More". You'll complete a brief assessment, schedule your first consultation, and we'll guide you through the entire onboarding process.

Is there a commitment period?

+

Most programs are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. The 90-Day Reset is a 3-month commitment to ensure you get the full benefit of the intensive program.

What kind of support is available?

+

Support varies by program. Most include coaching sessions, email support, and access to our community. Premium programs like Complete Wellness offer 24/7 support access for members.
